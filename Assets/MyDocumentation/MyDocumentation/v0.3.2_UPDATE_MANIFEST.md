# HEXA_766 v0.3.2 UPDATE MANIFEST

**Date:** 2026-09-29

## Milestone

Core Subsystem gameplay implementation.

## New Runtime Systems

- SubsystemController
- SubsystemFeedbackUI

## Gameplay Features

- Mass Dampener
- Ballast Injection
- Structural Reinforcement
- Network Stress
- One successful Subsystem per unit turn
- Failed attempts preserve Subsystem availability
- Effective Load limits of 1–5

## Targeting

- Renderer-based screen-space PlayerUnit targeting
- Expanded target bounds for reliable selection
- Minimum-load filtering for Mass Dampener
- Maximum-load filtering for Ballast Injection

## UI

GameUI now includes:

GameUI
└── SubsystemFeedbackPanel
    └── SubsystemFeedbackText

The panel displays themed HEXA_766 tactical feedback for successful,
invalid, and no-effect Subsystem actions.

## Verified

- Mass Dampener applies to allied units.
- Mass Dampener stops at Effective Load 1.
- Ballast Injection applies to enemy units.
- Ballast Injection stops at Effective Load 5.
- Structural Reinforcement modifies Capacity by +3.
- Network Stress modifies Capacity by -3.
- Invalid attempts do not consume the Subsystem.
- Subsystem feedback displays correctly.
- Unit targeting remains usable when robots are positioned close together.

## Preserved

The update does not intentionally alter:

- TurnManager turn sequencing
- movement
- pathfinding
- combat
- scoring
- objective lifecycle
- enemy phase
- collapse behavior
- 4v4 team structure
